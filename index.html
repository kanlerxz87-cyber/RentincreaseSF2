Answers to the five questions asked

QuestionAnswerDetail
What is a reasonable and normal increase in 94122 under local, state and federal law? Local law supplies the only operative ceiling: 1.6% annual, plus lawfully banked prior percentages. The state AB 1482 cap of 8.8% does not apply. Federal law sets no rent ceiling at all — only anti-discrimination limits. SF · State · Federal
How do large corporate owners handle increases, versus individual owners? The percentage is identical for both. The real differences are legal and administrative: corporate owners lose the Costa-Hawkins and AB 1482 entity exemptions, and algorithmic revenue-management pricing is banned outright in SF under Admin. Code § 37.10C. Individual owners differ mainly in administrative discipline — Unit 2's seven missed anniversaries are the classic individual-owner pattern. Owner Practices
What is the correct way to notify tenants, and what reasons are reasonable? Written notice, personally delivered or mailed (+5 days if mailed), 30 days at 10% or less and 90 days above 10%, stating the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date, and which portion is banked and its source dates. The annual allowable increase needs no justification at all — it exists by operation of law. Checklist
Would these increases survive review by an SF tenant lawyer? As computed, yes on arithmetic, notice and non-discrimination grounds — but not until 7 HIGH-severity flags are closed. The license question (F-22) voids everything if unanswered, and the retaliation screen (F-06) cannot be run without tenant records. Tenant-Lawyer Test
Raise one unit, or both, to avoid discrimination claims? Both, under one written policy — but at different, lawfully computed percentages. Do not raise only one without a documented neutral reason. Equally, do not equalise the percentages: forcing Unit 2 down forfeits 10.1% of lawful entitlement, and forcing Unit 1 up would be an unlawful increase outright. Anti-Discrimination
