Law-firm and property-manager summariesUseful for orientation, but several retrieved during research gave conflicting 2026 figures (one stated 1.7% for the same period in which the Rent Board publishes 1.6%). Every figure here comes from the Board's own Form 571 [sf.gov/documents/34344/571_Allowable_Annual_…]
Rent-increase calculator sitesSeveral present an "AGA" without stating which publication period it belongs to. The Board's rate is period- specific and changes each 1 March [sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent…]
HUD Fair Market RentsQueried; the FY2026 documentation system returned null values for San Francisco County. Not used. See F-08 [huduser.gov/portal/datasets/fmr.html]
News reporting on the DOJ settlementsUsed only to locate the Department of Justice releases, which are then cited directly [justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-requir…][justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-reache…]
Not legal advice. This site organises publicly available official sources so a property owner can check their own numbers. It is a research aid, not a legal opinion. Rent Ordinance questions should be put to a San Francisco Rent Board counselor (415-252-4600) and, for a tenancy-specific opinion, to a licensed attorney.
