San Francisco Rent Ordinance (Chapter 37)

Coverage: Buildings with Certificate of Occupancy before June 13, 1979 (1940 building qualifies) [Source: SF.gov]

Allowable Increase (2025-26): 1.4% [Source: SF.gov]

Allowable Increase (2026-27): 1.6% [Source: SF.gov]

Calculation: 60% of Bay Area CPI change (capped) [Source: SF.gov]

Notice Period: 30 days for increases under 10% [Source: SF.gov]

Banked Increases: Allowed up to 2 prior years (max 3 years total) [Source: SF.gov]

California AB 1482 (Tenant Protection Act)

Formula: 5% + Regional CPI, capped at 10% [Source: Calandlordtenantlaws]

Applicability: Backstop for units not covered by local ordinance (SF local law is stricter) [Source: Calandlordtenantlaws]

Notice Period: 30 days (≤10%) or 90 days (>10%) [Source: Calandlordtenantlaws]

Frequency: Max 2 increases per 12 months, combined cap applies [Source: Calandlordtenantlaws]

Federal Law (Fair Housing Act & HUD)

Rent Cap: No federal cap on private rents [Source: Commoner-law]

Anti-Discrimination: Prohibits increases based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, or disability [Source: Commoner-law]

Retaliation: Prohibits increases in retaliation for exercising legal rights [Source: Commoner-law]
