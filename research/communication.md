# Tenant Communication & Reasonable Reasons for Rent Increase

## Correct Way to Inform Tenants
- **Written Notice**: Must be in writing, delivered via approved methods (certified mail, personal delivery, etc.) [Source: SF Rent Board - Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/reports/march-2023/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) [Verified]
- **Notice Period**:
  - 30 days for increases under 10% [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
  - 90 days for increases of 10% or more (though SF cap is 1.4%/1.6%) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **SF Rent Board Forms**: Use official forms (e.g., Form 571 for allowable increases) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Content Requirements**: Must state the new rent amount, effective date, and basis for increase (e.g., 1.6% allowable increase) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]

## Reasonable Reasons for Rent Increase
- **Inflation-Based (CPI)**: 60% of Bay Area CPI change (1.4% for 2025-26, 1.6% for 2026-27) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Capital Improvements**: Requires Rent Board petition and approval (imputed interest rates apply) [Source: SF Rent Board - Capital Improvement Imputed Interest Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Operating Cost Increases**: May be petitioned for additional increase (requires documentation) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Property Tax Changes**: May be considered in petitions (requires documentation) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Banked Increases**: Accumulation from prior years (max 3 years total) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]

## SF Tenant Lawyer Scrutiny & Compliance
- **Review Criteria**:
  - Compliance with 1.4%/1.6% caps [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
  - Proper notice period and method [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
  - Absence of discrimination (Fair Housing Act) [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
  - Absence of retaliation [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
  - Documentation of reasonable reason (CPI, capital improvement, etc.) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Free Legal Services**: Tenants in SF have access to free lawyer services (e.g., Bay Area Legal Aid, Centro Legal de la Raza) [Source: RentCheckMe - Rent Control in San Francisco](https://rentreboot.com/guide/is-my-san-francisco-apartment-rent-controlled) [Verified]
- **Defense Strategy**: Consistent application of increases across comparable units, documented legitimate business reason, adherence to SF Rent Board caps [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
