Compliance Checklist and Notice Procedure

The fourteen elements a valid notice must satisfy, which reasons are legitimate, and the dated action plan.

The notice must contain and satisfy all fourteen elements

Rules § 4.10(b): “Any rent increase which does not conform with the provisions of this Section shall render the entire rent increase null and void, unless the amount requested equals no more than the allowable annual and banked rent increase(s).” There is a narrow good-faith cure where the overage is no more than half of one percent of prior base rent — an administrative law judge may readjust it — but get the notice right: the penalty for getting it wrong is total, not marginal.

One nuance: omitting the banked-portion breakdown alone does not void the increase under Rules § 4.12(b), whereas other defects do under § 4.10(b). Both provisions are published verbatim in Rules Part IV rather than reconciled, because they are in tension and it is not this project’s place to resolve an official conflict.

Practical drafting advice A landlord does not need to justify the annual allowable increase at all. Over-explaining invites argument; understating the banked breakdown voids the notice. State the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date, the banked portion and its source dates — and nothing else.
