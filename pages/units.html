Why the occupancy facts matter legally

FactLegal consequenceSource
Unit 1: an original occupant still resides in the unit Rent Board Rules § 6.14 and Costa-Hawkins market-rate resets are both unavailable. They operate only when the last original occupant vacates. Topic 153
Unit 1: an occupant was added Charging more for the added occupant is an unlawful rent increase even if the lease permits it. Tenants may petition to void it and recover refunds. Topic 152
Unit 1: two original occupants departed Fewer occupants is equally not a ground for any change. Tenants may generally replace departing roommates even where a lease prohibits it, subject to occupancy caps. Replacement of Roommates
Both units: tenancies are continuing Vacancy decontrol is unavailable. There is no limit on initial rent only for an empty unit. sf.gov
Both units: household composition is a known variable FEHA protects against discrimination based on the protected characteristic of a family member, friend, roommate or other associate — so any differential must be justified by tenure arithmetic, never by who lives there. CA Civil Rights Dept
