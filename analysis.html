Raise both by the same 1.6%Not unlawful for Unit 1; Unit 2 would leave banked increases unused.This is not the maximum lawful amount for Unit 2 and may be inconsistent with Unit 2's rent history. It is not required by law.sf.gov — Banked increases; sf.gov — Rent increases
Raise both by 13.1%No for Unit 1; exceeds Unit 1's 1.6% allowable.This would be an unlawful rent increase for Unit 1 unless a valid petition or other exception applies.sf.gov — Rent increases; sf.gov — Current Rates
Bottom line: No official source requires raising both units, and no official source permits applying one unit's percentage to another unit. Each unit is calculated from its own base rent, its own anniversary date, and its own banked balance. The lawful approach is to apply each unit's own verified rent history and allowable amount, document the reason, and avoid protected-class considerations. Raising both is not a substitute for correct calculations. sf.gov — Rent increases; sf.gov — Housing discrimination

Calculations: rules to keep in mind

  • No rounding up: Rent increases cannot be rounded up to the nearest dollar. sf.gov — Rent increases
  • No compounding: Banked increases are added, not compounded. sf.gov — Banked increases
  • Use the rate in effect on the effective date: The landlord should use the percentage in effect on the date of the increase, not the date the notice is served. sf.gov — Rent increases
  • Base rent only: Do not include passthroughs or fluctuating charges in the base rent calculation. sf.gov — Rent increases
  • License: A current rent increase license must be on file on the effective date; it comes from reporting into the Housing Inventory. sf.gov — Housing Inventory; sf.gov — §37.15
  • Notice: 30 days for ≤10%; 90 days for >10%; +5 days if mailed. Civ. Code §827
Next step: Verify the base rents, lease dates, rent history, build year, and license status. Then have a tenant lawyer review the final calculation and notice before service. sf.gov — Tenant and Landlord Resources; sf.gov — Rent Board counselor
