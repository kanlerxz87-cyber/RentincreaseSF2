First question: Rent Board license for an individual owner?

Yes. The Housing Inventory applies to owners of residential property, not only corporations. Reporting a tenant-occupied unit generates a Rent Increase License. A current license must be on file before an annual or banked increase takes effect. Official guidance describes annual Housing Inventory reporting plus a written tenant notice; it does not direct a separate Rent Board filing of each increase notice, and the license need not be attached to the tenant notice. Although the rule keys the license to the effective date rather than expressly to notice service, the prudent course is to verify the license in the Portal before serving notice. sf.gov — Housing Inventory; sf.gov — Rent increases

Time-critical: Unit 2’s service window closes 2026-11-30

Unit 2’s 13.1% requires an effective date on or before 2027-02-28 — the 2026–27 rate expires that day and the 2027–28 percentage is unpublished. A 90-day notice must therefore be served by 2026-11-30 (personal delivery) or 2026-11-25 (mail). Today (2026-11-10) the earliest reachable effective date is 2027-02-08 by personal service, or 2027-02-13 by mail. Unit 1 is not urgent: serve by 2027-01-02 for a 2027-02-01 effective date. Watch item: proposed 10% cap on banked / capital-improvement increases (File 261020) — not enacted

On 2026-11-09 the Rent Board posted a notice on the Current Rates, Banked rent increases and Learn about rent increases pages: the Board of Supervisors is considering File No. 261020 (status Pending Committee Action; introduced 2026-09-15; Land Use and Transportation Committee — not enacted and subject to change). Proposed § 37.3(h) (Leg Ver1) would bar banked (§ 37.3(a)(2)) and/or capital improvement (§ 37.3(a)(3)) increases “that cause a tenant’s rent to increase by more than a total of 10% of the lowest base rent that was in effect at any time during the 12 months immediately preceding the date the increase takes effect” through 2029-12-31 (excess deferred to on or after 2030-01-01), applying retroactively to banked notices provided on or after 2026-09-15 and capital-improvement petitions filed on or after that date, and would amend § 37.15(b)(2)(F) to report prior-12-month banked increases. Nothing changes under current law: Unit 2’s 13.1% is lawful if the ledger is right. If enacted as drafted, 13.1% would exceed the cap, whereas the phased 10.0% option ($315.00 → $3,465.00, 30-day notice) stays within 10% total under every reading (see F-27 on the narrower title-only 11.6% arithmetic vs. literal § 37.3(h) text). Check Legistar status immediately before serving any Unit 2 notice. Legistar — File 261020 · Leg Ver1 · sf.gov — Current Rates (notice 2026-11-09) · Flagged for Review (F-27)

Project scope

This dashboard collects, organizes, and analyzes the requirements for raising rent on two rent-controlled units in a 1940 building in San Francisco's Outer Sunset (ZIP 94122). The 1940 build year, lease dates, rents, and occupancy are owner-supplied intake facts and are marked unverified until confirmed against official records. Tenant names and other personal identifiers are withheld; dates and dollar amounts are preserved as provided.

Privacy: No tenant names, initials, or contact details are published. The site uses placeholders only. Personal information is kept internal.

How to use this dashboard

  • Executive Summary — the bottom line in one page, starting with the Rent Board license question.
  • Units (Anonymized) — intake facts, verification status, and calculations.
  • Increase Analysis — Unit 1 and Unit 2 date-specific scenarios, including banked increases.
  • Occupancy & Roommates — Unit 1’s household change under Rules § 6.14, Costa-Hawkins, and the additional-occupant ban.
  • Laws & Regulations — local, state, and federal rules with official links.
  • Notice & Process — the correct way to inform tenants and permissible reasons.
  • Tenant Protections — just cause, free legal help, discrimination, retaliation, and a lawyer-review checklist.
  • Owner Practices — corporate vs. individual owner practices and what official sources do and do not say.
  • Action Checklist — Gate 0 prerequisites, computed service deadlines, notice content, and pre-service review.
  • Flagged for Review — every unverified item and irregularity (F-01 to F-32).
  • Sources — all official sources used.
  • Banking Interval (F-16) — F-16 resolved on the evidence, independent re-verification, and service-window countdowns.
