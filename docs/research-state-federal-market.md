Code §§ 1954.50–1954.53`) | Authorizes vacancy decontrol (market-rate initial rent on true turnover) and exempts post-Feb 1, 1995 construction and separately alienable single-family homes/condos (`§ 1954.52`). Under **`§ 1954.53(d)(2)–(3)`**, a market-rate reset is **strictly barred** during partial occupancy changes where **one or more original occupants remain in lawful possession** (directly protecting Unit 1 while `Tenant 1A` resides there). | [Cal. | **California Tenant Protection Act of 2019 — AB 1482** (`Cal. Civ. Code § 1947.12`) | Caps statewide annual rent increases at `5% + regional CPI` (max `10%`), which the CA Attorney General publishes for San Francisco County as **`6.3%`** (`8/1/2025–7/31/2026`) and **`8.8%`** (`8/1/2026–7/31/2027`). **Crucially**, under `§ 1947.12(d)(3)` and CA DOJ guidance, **San Francisco's stricter local Chapter 37 cap (`1.6%` for `3/1/2026–2/28/2027`) supersedes AB 1482** for pre-June 13, 1979 multi-unit buildings. | [CA Attorney General — Limits on Rent Increases](https://oag.ca.gov/rentcaps) · [Cal. | **Mandatory Written Notice & Delivery Rules** (`Cal. Civ. Code § 827(b)`) | Rent increases require formal **written** notice (phone, text, or email alone is invalid). **30 days' prior written notice** is required if the cumulative increase within 12 months is **≤ 10%** (`§ 827(b)(2)`); **90 days' prior written notice** is required if the cumulative increase within 12 months is **> 10%** (`§ 827(b)(3)(A)`). Service must be by personal delivery (`§ 827(b)(1)(A)`) or mail under `CCP § 1013` (`§ 827(b)(1)(B)`). | [Cal. Civ. Code § 827](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=827.) · [CA Attorney General — Landlord-Tenant Issues](https://oag.ca.gov/tenants) |
| **Service by Mail +5 Calendar Days Extension** (`Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1013(a)`) | When a rent increase notice is served by first-class mail within California, the statutory notice period is extended by **5 calendar days** (**35 days** total for increases ≤ 10%; **95 days** total for increases > 10%). | [Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 1013](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1013.&lawCode=CCP) |
| **Anti-Retaliation Protections** (`Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5`) | Prohibits increasing rent, decreasing services, or attempting eviction within **180 days** (or at any time with retaliatory motive under `§ 1942.5(d)`) after a tenant makes a good-faith repair/habitability complaint, contacts an inspection agency, or exercises legal tenant rights. Carries statutory damages and mandatory prevailing-party attorney's fees (`§ 1942.5(i)`). | [Cal. Civ. Code § 1942.5](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=1942.5.&lawCode=CIV) |
| **CA Fair Employment and Housing Act — FEHA** (`Cal. Gov. Code § 12955`) | Prohibits differential rent increases, inferior terms, harassment, or retaliation based on race, color, ancestry, national origin, citizenship, immigration status, primary language, religion, mental/physical disability, sex, gender, gender identity/expression, sexual orientation, genetic information, marital status, familial status, **source of income (including Section 8 / vouchers)**, military/veteran status, or age. Applies fully to 2-unit rentals (no duplex "Mrs. Murphy" exemption). | [Cal. Gov. Code § 12955](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=12955.) · [CA Civil Rights Dept. — Housing Discrimination](https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/housing/) |
| **Unruh Civil Rights Act** (`Cal. Civ. Code § 51`) | Residential landlords operate "business establishments" under California law and may not engage in arbitrary, invidious, or protected-class discrimination in rental pricing or lease terms. | [Cal. Civ. Code § 51](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=CIV&sectionNum=51.) |
| **Emergency Anti-Price-Gouging Law** (`Cal. Penal Code § 396`) | Caps rental price increases at 10% during proclaimed states of emergency, while explicitly preserving **stricter local rent-control ceilings** such as SF's 1.6% annual cap. | [CA Attorney General — Housing & Penal Code § 396](https://oag.ca.gov/housing) |

## 2. Federal Housing, Civil Rights, & Antitrust Laws

| Federal Authority | Verified Legal Rule & Impact on SF Rent Increases | Official Primary Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal Fair Housing Act (Title VIII)** (`42 U.S.C. §§ 3601–3619`; `24 C.F.R. Part 100`) | Prohibits discrimination in the **terms, conditions, or privileges of rental** (`42 U.S.C. § 3604(b)`) and discriminatory statements (`§ 3604(c)`) or interference/retaliation (`§ 3617`) based on **race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, and disability**. Note: Even if an owner-occupied ≤4-unit building claimed the narrow federal `§ 3603(b)(2)` exemption, `§ 3604(c)`, `42 U.S.C. §§ 1981–1982`, and California FEHA/Unruh still strictly prohibit any discrimination. | [HUD — Housing Discrimination Under the Fair Housing Act](https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/fair-housing-act-overview) · [42 U.S.C. | **Federal Rent Control Preemption Status** | Federal law imposes **no general ceiling or preemption** on local/state residential rent control for private unsubsidized properties; local caps (`1.6%` in SF) govern so long as constitutional due process and federal civil-rights statutes are respected. | [HUD — Fair Housing Overview](https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/fair-housing-act-overview) · [CA Attorney General — Rent Caps](https://oag.ca.gov/rentcaps) |

Mirrors San Francisco's local algorithmic pricing ban (`SF Admin. Code § 37.10C`). | [DOJ — Sues RealPage (Aug 2024)](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/justice-department-sues-realpage-algorithmic-pricing-scheme-harms-millions-american-renters) · [DOJ — Sues Six Large Landlords (Jan 2025)](https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/justice-department-sues-six-large-landlords-algorithmic-pricing-scheme-harms-millions) · [DOJ — RealPage Proposed Settlement (Nov 2025)](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/justice-department-requires-realpage-end-sharing-competitively-sensitive-information-and) |
