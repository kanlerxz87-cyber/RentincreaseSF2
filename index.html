Project scope

This site collects, organizes, and analyzes the requirements for raising rent on two rent-controlled units in a 1940 building in San Francisco's Outer Sunset (94122). All personal information is anonymized with placeholder names; dates and dollar amounts are preserved unchanged. Every figure is verified against official sources — see each table's source links. Anything that could not be confirmed from an official source is left blank and listed on the Flagged for Review page.
