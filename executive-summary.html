Executive Summary

Property: Two-unit residential building, built 1940, San Francisco Outer Sunset, ZIP 94122. Because the building predates June 13, 1979, both units are rent controlled under the San Francisco Rent Ordinance (Administrative Code Chapter 37). sf.gov — Rent increases

Reasonable and normal increase: The San Francisco Rent Board's allowable annual increase for rent-controlled units is 1.6% effective March 1, 2026 through February 28, 2027 (based on 60% of the Bay Area CPI change). Increases are limited to once every 12 months. sf.gov — Current Rates sf.gov — Learn about rent increases

Unit 1 (base rent $3,995/mo): maximum annual increase of $63.92/mo → new rent $4,058.92/mo.

Unit 2 (base rent $3,150/mo): maximum annual increase of $50.40/mo → new rent $3,200.40/mo.

How to inform tenants: Written notice at least 30 days before the increase takes effect (90 days if the increase, alone or combined, exceeds 10%; add 5 days if mailed). The notice should state the dollar amount, the percentage, the effective date, and any banked portion. A current rent increase license from the Rent Board is required before imposing an increase. sf.gov sf.gov — Banked increases

Fairness / discrimination avoidance: Apply the identical policy (same allowable percentage, same notice, same effective-date rules) to both units. Treating one unit differently without a documented, lawful, non-discriminatory reason creates risk under the federal Fair Housing Act (42 U.S.C. §3604) and California law. HUD.gov

Reasonable reasons for an increase: The annual CPI-based allowable increase is itself the legally recognized, normal-business-practice reason; no further justification is required as long as it stays within the cap. Anything above the cap requires a Rent Board petition (e.g., capital improvements, operating costs). sf.gov
