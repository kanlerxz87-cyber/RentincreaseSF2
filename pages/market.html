What this does and does not establish Market position is irrelevant to the Rent Ordinance calculation. No official SF source treats comparability as a ground for an annual increase during a continuing tenancy; going above the annual allowable requires a Rent Board petition. These figures matter for two other reasons only: hardship exposure (a tenant may seek relief from a passthrough, and 42.1% cost-burden means a large increase lands on households with little headroom) and petition risk (an increase that moves a household from below to above the ZIP median is more likely to be challenged, and defending a challenge costs more than the increase is worth). That is the practical case for the phased 10% option on Unit 2.
