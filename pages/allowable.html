The complete official percentage history, transcribed verbatim from SF Rent Board Info Sheet 571.

Current allowable increase: 1.6% Effective 2026-03-01 through 2027-02-28. SF.gov announcement, 2026-01-14

The Rent Board calculates this each year as 60% of the increase in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers in the Bay Area, and in no event more than 7% (SF Admin. Code § 37.3(a)(1)). The table below is transcribed verbatim from the official Rent Board Info Sheet 571, cross-checked against the SF.gov Current Rates report.

How to use it. Find the tenant’s anniversary date, identify which effective period contains it, and take that period’s percentage. Where several anniversaries were skipped, add the percentages — never compound them — provided 12 full months have elapsed on each (Rent Board Rules § 4.12; see Flag F-03 on a conflict between two official sources). Use the percentage in effect on the effective date of the increase, not the date the notice is served.
