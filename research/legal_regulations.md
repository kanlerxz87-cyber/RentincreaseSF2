# Legal & Regulatory Research: San Francisco Outer Sunset (94122)

## Local Law: SF Rent Ordinance (Chapter 37)
- **Source**: [SF.gov - Current Rates, including Rent Increase, Relocation, Sec. Deposit](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Coverage**: Buildings with Certificate of Occupancy before June 13, 1979 (1940 building qualifies) [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Allowable Increase (March 1, 2025 – Feb 28, 2026)**: 1.4% [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Allowable Increase (March 1, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027)**: 1.6% [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Calculation Formula**: 60% of Bay Area CPI change (capped) [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Notice Period**: 30 days for increases under 10% [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)
- **Banked Increases**: Allowed up to 2 prior years (max 3 years total) [1](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit)

## State Law: California AB 1482 (Tenant Protection Act)
- **Source**: [California Rent Cap 2026: AB 1482 Rent Increase Limits](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482) [Verified]
- **Formula**: 5% + Regional CPI, capped at 10% [2](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482)
- **Applicability**: Backstop for units not covered by local ordinance (SF local ordinance is stricter) [2](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482)
- **Notice Period**: 30 days (≤10%) or 90 days (>10%) [2](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482)
- **Frequency**: Max 2 increases per 12 months, combined cap applies [2](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482)

## Federal Law: Fair Housing Act & HUD Regulations
- **Source**: [Rent Control Laws by State (2026): Increase Limits](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
- **Rent Cap**: No federal cap on private rents [3](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control)
- **Anti-Discrimination**: Prohibits increases based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, or disability [3](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control)
- **Retaliation**: Prohibits increases in retaliation for exercising legal rights [3](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control)
- **Subsidized Housing**: Federal regulations apply to Section 8/Public Housing (not applicable here unless specified) [3](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control)
