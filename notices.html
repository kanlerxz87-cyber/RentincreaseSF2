Notice content, service rules and deadlines

What a San Francisco rent increase notice must contain, how it must be served, and the calendar for both units — including the 90-day constraint that governs Unit 2.

The six things every notice must contain

These come from three places at once: the Rent Ordinance requires the component breakdown [sf.gov/information--sec-373-rent-limitations], Rule 4.12 requires the banked portion and its base dates [sf.gov/reports/december-2022/rent-board-rule…], and Civil Code § 827 requires the increase itself [leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displ…]. The Rent Board's own summary requires all of them on the notice [sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-franci…].

1. The dollar amountThe increase in dollars, and the new base rent.
2. The percentageThe percentage of the increase.
3. The effective dateThe date the new rent takes effect.
4. The annual portionWhich part is the annual allowable increase.
5. The banked portionWhich part is banked, and the dates that banked amount is based on (include the percentage for each year).
6. Any passthroughWhich part, if any, is a gas/electric, water-bond, GO-bond or capital-improvement passthrough. On these figures there is none.
Why item 5 matters more than it looks Rule 4.12(b) says failure to state the banked portion and its base dates does not by itself void the increase. But § 37.3(b)(5) says any increase that does not conform with § 37.3 is null and void, and § 37.3(b) requires the landlord to inform the tenant in writing which portion is annual and which is banked [sf.gov/reports/december-2022/rent-board-rule…][sf.gov/information--sec-373-rent-limitations]. Where the two read together, the safe course is to include the breakdown. It costs nothing and removes the argument entirely.

Notice period and service

30 daysWhere the increase is 10% or less of the rent charged at any time in the 12 months before the effective date [leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displ…]
90 daysWhere the increase is more than 10% — this is where Unit 2 lands at 13.1% [leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displ…]
+5 days if mailedIf the notice is served by mail, add five days to the notice period [sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-franci…][leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displ…]
How to servePersonal delivery to the tenant, or mail under Code of Civil Procedure § 1013 [leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displ…]
Which rate to quoteThe percentage in effect on the effective date, not the one in effect when the notice is served [sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-franci…]
LicenceA current rent increase licence must be on file on the effective date. It does not have to be attached to the notice [sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory]

The Unit 2 squeeze A 13.1% increase needs 90 days. To take effect before the 1.6% period ends on 28 February 2027, the notice must be served in November 2026. If that window is missed, the increase either takes effect later — at whatever rate the Board publishes for March 2027 onward for the annual portion, with the banked 11.5% unchanged — or the owner takes a smaller increase now to stay under 10% and keep the 30-day rule. Both are lawful; the second is usually the cheaper commercial outcome.

Draft notice — Unit 1

Template wording, not a legal document. Fill the bracketed fields and have it checked before service. The structure deliberately mirrors the six required elements.

NOTICE OF RENT INCREASE
[Property address] · San Francisco, CA 94122
Date of notice: [date of service]

To: [Tenant name(s)] and all other occupants
Unit: Unit 1
Current base rent: $3,995.00 per month
New base rent:    $4,114.85 per month
Increase:         $119.85 per month (3.0%)
Effective date:   1 February 2027

This increase is authorised by the San Francisco Residential Rent
Stabilization and Arbitration Ordinance (Administrative Code
Chapter 37) and consists of the following parts:

  (a) Annual allowable increase ....... 1.6%   = $63.92
      Allowed under Section 37.3(a)(1) for increases effective
      between 1 March 2026 and 28 February 2027, published by the
      San Francisco Rent Board.

  (b) Banked increase ................. 1.4%   = $55.93
      The allowable annual increase of 1.4% for the period
      1 March 2025 to 28 February 2026, which became available on
      1 February 2026 and was not imposed. Accumulated under
      Section 37.3(a)(2) and Rent Board Rules and Regulations
      Section 4.12 and imposed on 1 February 2027.

  Total increase: 3.0% = $119.85 per month

No portion of this increase is a passthrough of utility charges,
water revenue bond charges, general obligation bond charges, or
capital improvement costs.

The base rent is increased only. No charge is made for any change
in the number of occupants.

Payable in accordance with your existing rental agreement. Your
security deposit is unaffected.

Your landlord holds a current rent increase licence for this unit,
issued by the San Francisco Rent Board following Housing Inventory
reporting under Section 37.15.

Questions about this notice may be directed to the San Francisco
Rent Board, 25 Van Ness Avenue, Suite 700, San Francisco, CA 94102,
(415) 252-4600. A tenant may petition the Rent Board regarding an
alleged unlawful rent increase; there is no filing fee.

[Owner name] · [Owner contact]

Draft notice — Unit 2 (banked component table)

Unit 2's notice needs the same six elements, but the banked portion must be broken out by year because it spans six anniversary dates. This is the part a petition attacks first.

NOTICE OF RENT INCREASE
[Property address] · San Francisco, CA 94122
Date of notice: [serve on or before 17 November 2026]

To: [Tenant name(s)] and all other occupants
Unit: Unit 2
Current base rent: $3,150.00 per month
New base rent:    $3,562.65 per month
Increase:         $412.65 per month (13.1%)
Effective date:   15 February 2027

Notice period: this increase exceeds 10% of the rent charged in the
12 months before the effective date, and is therefore given with at
least 90 days' written notice as required by California Civil Code
Section 827(b)(3).

  (a) Banked increases accumulated under Section 37.3(a)(2) and
      Rent Board Rules and Regulations Section 4.12 — 11.5%:

        1.8%  available 1 June 2020, not imposed
        0.7%  available 1 June 2021, not imposed
        2.3%  available 1 June 2022, not imposed
        3.6%  available 1 June 2023, not imposed
        1.7%  available 1 June 2024, not imposed
        1.4%  available 1 June 2025, not imposed

      These percentages are added together, not compounded, and are
      applied to the current base rent: 11.5% of $3,150.00 = $362.25

  (b) Annual allowable increase — 1.6%:
      Allowed under Section 37.3(a)(1) for increases effective
      between 1 March 2026 and 28 February 2027.
      1.6% of $3,150.00 = $50.40

  Total: 11.5% + 1.6% = 13.1% of $3,150.00 = $412.65 per month

No portion of this increase is a passthrough of utility charges,
water revenue bond charges, general obligation bond charges, or
capital improvement costs.

The base rent is increased as an annual and banked increase only.
No charge is made for any change in the number of occupants. Your
security deposit is unaffected.

Your landlord holds a current rent increase licence for this unit,
issued by the San Francisco Rent Board following Housing Inventory
reporting under Section 37.15.

[Owner name] · [Owner contact]

Sanity check before sending: $3,150.00 × 11.5% = $362.25 and $3,150.00 × 1.6% = $50.40; $362.25 + $50.40 = $412.65. The two parts must sum to the stated total exactly — if they do not, the notice is internally inconsistent and will be attacked on that alone.
