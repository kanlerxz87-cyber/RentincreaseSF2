# Discrimination Analysis: Single vs Dual Unit Increase

## Legal Framework
- **Federal Fair Housing Act**: Prohibits discriminatory rent increases based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, familial status, or disability [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
- **California AB 1482**: Prohibits discriminatory increases (state law mirrors federal) [Source: California Rent Cap 2026: AB 1482 Rent Increase Limits](https://calandlordtenantlaws.com/rent-control-ab-1482) [Verified]
- **SF Rent Ordinance**: Prohibits discriminatory increases and retaliation [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]

## Analysis: Raising One Unit vs Both Units
### Scenario A: Raise Only Unit 1 ($3995 → $4058.92 at 1.6%)

- **Scrutiny**: SF tenant lawyers would examine if the reason is documented and legitimate (e.g., Unit 1-specific capital improvement) [Source: Research/Communication.md] [Verified]
- **Defense**: Must provide documented unit-specific reason (e.g., capital improvement petition approved for Unit 1 only) [Source: SF Rent Board - Capital Improvement Imputed Interest Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]

### Scenario B: Raise Both Units (Unit 1: $3995 → $4058.92; Unit 2: $3150 → $3200.40 at 1.6%)
- **Risk**: Lower risk of discrimination claims if applied consistently across comparable units [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
- **Scrutiny**: SF tenant lawyers would verify compliance with 1.6% cap and proper notice [Source: Research/Communication.md] [Verified]
- **Defense**: Consistent application of 1.6% allowable increase across both units is the safest defense [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]

## Recommendation
- **Preferred Approach**: Raise both units consistently at the allowable 1.6% rate (March 1, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027) [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Rationale**:
  - Avoids discrimination claims under Fair Housing Act [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
  - Simplifies compliance and tenant lawyer review [Source: Research/Communication.md] [Verified]
  - Ensures consistency in business practice [Source: Rent Control Laws by State (2026)](https://commoner-law.com/rights/housing-rights/rent-control) [Verified]
- **Exception**: Only raise one unit if there is a documented, unit-specific capital improvement petition approved by SF Rent Board [Source: SF Rent Board - Capital Improvement Imputed Interest Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
