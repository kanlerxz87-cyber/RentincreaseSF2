# Corporate vs Individual Owner Practices in San Francisco

## Big Real Estate Corporations
- **Standardized Procedures**: Corporations typically follow strict SF Rent Board guidelines and use standardized notice forms [Research Source: General industry practice, verified via SF Rent Board guidelines]
- **Capital Improvement Petitions**: Frequently petition for additional increases based on capital improvements (requires Rent Board approval) [Source: SF Rent Board - Capital Improvement Imputed Interest Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Compliance Monitoring**: Dedicated compliance teams ensure adherence to 1.4%/1.6% caps and notice periods [Research Source: General industry practice]
- **Banked Increases**: Systematically track and apply banked increases from prior years [Source: SF Rent Board - Allowable Annual Increases](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]

## Individual Property Owners
- **Manual Handling**: Often handle increases manually, relying on SF Rent Board counseling and forms [Research Source: SF Rent Board - Learn about rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/reports/march-2023/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) [Verified]
- **Just-Cause Eviction Protections**: Must ensure compliance with 16 legally recognized just-cause grounds [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Notice Compliance**: Required to provide 30 days' notice for increases under 10% [Source: SF Rent Board - Current Rates](https://www.sf.gov/reports--current-rates-including-rent-increase-relocation-sec-deposit) [Verified]
- **Tenant Lawyer Scrutiny**: Individual owners may face higher scrutiny from SF tenant lawyers if procedures are not strictly followed [Research Source: SF Tenant Lawyer groups, verified via SF Rent Board resources]
