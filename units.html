Important notes

  • Unit 1 (Tenant 1A + Tenant 1D): April 1, 2026 @ $3,995/mo was the continuing-tenancy / occupant change 14 months after the February 1, 2025 lease start. Because original co-tenant Tenant 1A remains in occupancy, Costa-Hawkins / Rules §6.14 market reset is prohibited. Confirm the April 1, 2026 document did not create an unexpired 12-month fixed term through March 31, 2027. Prior to February 1, 2027, the maximum prospective increase is 1.6% ($63.92 → $4,058.92) because less than 24 full months have passed since Feb 1, 2025. sf.gov — Rules §6.14; sf.gov — Banked increases
  • Unit 1 alternative (Feb 1, 2027): Waiting until February 1, 2027 (24 full months) banks 1.4% + 1.6% = 3.0% (+$119.85/mo → $4,114.85/mo). sf.gov — Irregular periods
  • Unit 2 (Tenant 2A + Tenant 2B): Standard current-year 1.6% = +$50.40/mo ($3,200.40/mo). The 13.1% (+$412.65/mo → $3,562.65/mo) is a calculated maximum assuming zero prior increases since June 1, 2019; shifting the effective date into March 1–May 31, 2027 forfeits June 1, 2026's 1.6% under Topic 503. sf.gov — Banked increases
  • Unit 2 notice: Because 13.1% exceeds 10%, written notice must be at least 90 days, plus 5 calendar days if mailed inside California (95 days total; 95 days from Nov 6, 2026 = Feb 9, 2027). Civ. Code §827; CCP §1013
  • Additional occupant: A landlord cannot charge additional rent simply because an additional occupant or replacement roommate moves in with an existing original tenant. sf.gov — Rent increases
  • Rent Board Fee ($29.50 recovery): Separate from base rent, landlords may bill 50% ($29.50) of the $59.00 annual apartment Rent Board fee if itemized separately. sf.gov — Rent Board Fee
