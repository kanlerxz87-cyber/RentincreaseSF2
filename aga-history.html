How to read this table for banking

The rate attached to a given anniversary is the rate in effect on that date, not today's rate. A year skipped when the allowance was 1.4% banks 1.4%, not 1.6% [sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases]. That is why Unit 2's six banked years use six different percentages rather than 1.6% six times.

To find the rate for a particular anniversary, read down the "Effective from" column to the 1 March period that covers the anniversary date. An anniversary of 1 June 2023 falls in the 1 March 2023 – 29 February 2024 period, so the rate is 3.6%.

The 2026 anomaly, in the Board's own words

Rule 1.12 was amended on 13 January 2026 because the federal government shutdown of 2025 prevented the Bureau of Labor Statistics from ever publishing the October 2025 CPI. Because the Ordinance requires an October-to-October comparison, the Board approximated October 2025 as the geometric mean of the published August 2025 and December 2025 values. The Bureau confirms the October 2025 figure does not exist [media.api.sf.gov/documents/626A_Complete_Lis…][bls.gov/regions/west/news-release/consumerpr…].

The Board has said a similar interim method will be needed for the 2027 figure, after which the standard calculation resumes. The rate for March 2027 onward was not published as at 6 November 2026 and is deliberately not shown here.
