Project scope

This dashboard collects, organizes, and analyzes the legal and procedural requirements for raising rent on two residential units in a reported 1940 building in San Francisco's Outer Sunset (ZIP 94122) as of November 6, 2026. The reported 1940 build year, lease dates, base rents, and occupancy history are intake facts whose independent public-record confirmation is left blank and marked unverified until confirmed against official records. All personal identifiers are strictly withheld and replaced with anonymized placeholders (Tenant 1A, Tenant 1B, Tenant 1C, Tenant 1D for Unit 1; Tenant 2A, Tenant 2B for Unit 2) while preserving exact dates and dollar amounts.

Privacy: Zero tenant names, initials, street addresses, or contact details are published anywhere on this site or in repository files. Anonymized placeholders are used exclusively.
Not legal advice: This is a compliance review based on official sources. The SF Rent Board cannot give legal advice. Consult a tenant lawyer or a Rent Board counselor before serving a notice.

How to use this dashboard

  • Executive Summary — the bottom line in one page.
  • Units (Anonymized) — intake facts, verification status, and calculations.
  • Increase Analysis — Unit 1 and Unit 2 calculations, including banked increases.
  • Laws & Regulations — local, state, and federal rules with official links.
  • Notice & Process — the correct way to inform tenants and permissible reasons.
  • Tenant Protections — just cause, free legal help, discrimination, and a lawyer-review checklist.
  • Owner Practices — corporate vs. individual owner practices and what official sources do and do not say.
  • Flagged for Review — every unverified item and irregularity.
  • Sources — all official sources used.
