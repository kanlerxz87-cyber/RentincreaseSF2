The Tenant-Lawyer Scrutiny Test

Free tenant legal services in San Francisco, the petition routes a tenant can use, and the eight-question review an increase must survive.

The premise is correct. San Francisco tenants have real, free, well-organised representation, and the right way to design an increase is to assume it will be read by a competent tenant attorney — then build the file so that attorney concludes it was ordinary, lawful business practice.

All forms from the official Rent Board Forms Center. Form 525 from the official Form 525 PDF.

The two that matter most here Form 518 can stop an increase before it takes effect if the notice is unlawful on its face — a defective notice is not merely void later, it can be blocked in advance. Form 516C recalculates lawful base rent from every increase since April 1, 1982, which is why the complete Unit 2 ledger (Flag F-08) and any prior-owner records must be assembled before noticing, not after a petition arrives.

The eight-question test

A tenant attorney will work through roughly this. Every answer must be documented before the notice goes out, not reconstructed afterwards.

Conclusion An increase that answers all eight cleanly is — in the terms the owner asked about — reasonable, normal business practice, non-discriminatory and lawful. As computed on this site, questions 4 (arithmetic), 6 (notice) and 8 (non-discrimination) are already satisfied and documented. Questions 1, 2, 3, 5 and 7 are open and must be closed before service. An increase that fails question 2 fails completely regardless of the rest, because Rent Board Rules § 13.14 renders it null and void in its entirety.

How to make the file review-ready

  1. Print and retain the Info Sheet 571 in force on the effective date, with the date of download noted.
  2. Retain a screenshot or export from portal.sfrb.org showing the rent increase license in effect on the effective date.
  3. Retain the computation sheet per unit: base rent, each skipped anniversary with its official percentage, the 12-month elapse date for each, the addition, the multiplication, and the result.
  4. Retain the § 1942.5 retaliation screen with a dated nil return if there is nothing to report.
  5. Retain proof of service — personal delivery receipt or certified mail record — and the exact notice template used for both units.
  6. Retain the dated written uniform rent-increase policy.
  7. Keep a contemporaneous file note recording the business reason for any differential treatment. Never record anything about a protected characteristic.
