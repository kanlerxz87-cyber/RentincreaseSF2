## 3. Mandatory Housing Inventory Reporting & Rent Increase License (`SF Admin. Code § 37.15`)

- Effective **March 1, 2023** for buildings with **1–9 residential units** (with annual updates due every **March 1** at `https://portal.sfrb.org`), residential property owners **must** submit unit occupancy and base-rent bracket data to the SF Rent Board Housing Inventory to obtain a **Rent Increase License**.
- Under `SF Admin. Code § 37.15(e)` and Rent Board Rules & Regulations `§ 13.14`, **a current Rent Increase License must be on file before any annual (`§ 37.3(a)(1)`) or banked (`§ 37.3(a)(2)`) rent increase goes into effect**. Any annual or banked increase taking effect while unlicensed is **null and void**, and tenants may check license status online via the Rent Board Portal and file an **Unlawful Rent Increase Petition** or **Tenant Summary Petition**.
- **Sources:** [SF Admin. Code § 37.15](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-16540) · [SF.gov — The Rent Board Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/rent-board-housing-inventory) · [SF.gov — Notice of Owner Noncompliance with Housing Inventory](https://www.sf.gov/notice-of-owner-noncompliance-with-housing-inventory)

## 4. Banking of Skipped Annual Increases (`SF Admin. Code § 37.3(a)(2)` & Topic 503)

- **24-Month / 12-Full-Months-After-Skip Rule:** A skipped annual increase is banked **only** if at least **12 full months elapse after the skipped anniversary date** (i.e., 24 full months pass without any rent increase). If the landlord changes the anniversary date before 12 full months pass after the most recent skipped anniversary date, that intermediate skipped increase is **permanently lost**.
- **Simple Addition Only (No Compounding or Proration):** Banked percentages are added linearly (`sum of banked annual %s + current effective annual %`) and multiplied against current base rent. Compounding is strictly unlawful.
- **No Petition Needed, Strict Notice Disclosure Required:** Landlords do not need a Rent Board petition to impose banked increases, **provided** they hold an active Rent Increase License and serve written notice stating the dollar increase, total percentage, effective date, and **which portion/dates of the increase are banked** (`SF Admin. Code § 37.3(b)(1)`).
- **Rate on Effective Date Controls:** The landlord must apply the annual rate in effect on the **effective date** of the increase, not the date the notice is served.
- **Sources:** [SF.gov — Banked rent increases](https://www.sf.gov/information--banked-rent-increases) · [SF.gov — Learn about rent increases in San Francisco](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco) · [SF Admin. Code § 37.3(a)(2)](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-70881)

## 5. Co-Tenants, Replacement Roommates, & Subsequent Occupants (`Rules § 6.14` & Costa-Hawkins)

- **No Rent Increase for Additional/Replacement Roommates:** A landlord **cannot** charge extra rent or reset base rent merely because a new or replacement roommate (`Tenant 1D`) moves in with an existing original occupant (`Tenant 1A`), even if a lease clause attempts to authorize it ([SF.gov — Learn about rent increases in San Francisco](https://www.sf.gov/learn-about-rent-increases-san-francisco)).
- **Original Occupant Still in Possession Blocks Market Reset:** Both `Cal. Civ. Code § 1954.53(d)` (Costa-Hawkins) and `SF Rent Board Rules & Regulations § 6.14` allow a market-rate reset **only when ALL original occupants no longer permanently reside in / have vacated the unit**. Because `Tenant 1A` (an initial tenant from the `February 1, 2025` lease) remains in Unit 1 as of `April 1, 2026`, Unit 1 remains a **protected continuing tenancy** capped at the SF Rent Board annual rate.
- **Co-Tenant Direct-Relationship Consequence (`Tenant 1D`):** Under official SF Rent Board guidance, a **co-tenant** who has a direct written/oral rental agreement with the landlord (or from whom the landlord directly accepts rent) is **exempt from Costa-Hawkins unlimited increases** regardless of move-in date, and can only be subject to a future `Rules § 6.14` increase after the last original occupant vacates **if** the landlord timely served a written **`§ 6.14` Notice** within a reasonable time (rebuttably 60 days) of learning of occupancy.
- **Sources:** [SF.gov — Rent Increases Under Section 6.14 And Costa-Hawkins](https://www.sf.gov/information--rent-increases-under-section-614-and-costa-hawkins) · [SF Rent Board Rules & Regulations Part VI (§§ 6.14–6.15E)](https://www.sf.gov/reports--december-2022--rent-board-rules-and-regulations-part-vi-rent-increase-justifications) · [Tenant Petition Form C (516C)](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/516C_Tenant_Petition_Form_C_EN_5.25_Fillable_Updated.pdf)

## 6. Algorithmic Pricing Ban, Harassment Protections, Passthroughs & Free Tenant Legal Services

- **Prohibition on Algorithmic Rent-Setting Devices (`SF Admin. Code § 37.10C`):** Effective **October 14, 2024**, San Francisco prohibits the sale or use of algorithmic devices analyzing nonpublic competitor data to set rents or manage occupancy levels in SF residential units ([SF.gov News — Oct 16, 2024](https://www.sf.gov/news--new-law-prohibits-algorithmic-devices-used-set-rents-san-francisco)).
- **Tenant Harassment (`SF Admin. Code § 37.10B`) & Just Cause/Anti-Retaliation (`§ 37.9`):** Bad-faith rent demands, failure to perform repairs, privacy violations, or retaliatory acts expose landlords to Rent Board rent-reduction petitions (for housing service decreases), misdemeanor liability, and civil treble damages plus mandatory tenant attorney's fees ([SF Admin. Code § 37.10B](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-16480), [SF Admin. Code § 37.9](https://codelibrary.amlegal.com/codes/san_francisco/latest/sf_admin/0-0-0-16273)).
- **Petitions & Passthroughs (`SF Admin. Code §§ 37.7, 37.8`):** Capital Improvement and Operating & Maintenance increases require prior Rent Board petitions; General Obligation Bond and Water Revenue Bond passthroughs require official Rent Board calculation worksheets served with the notice and **never** enter base rent ([SF.gov — Landlord Petitions and Passthroughs](https://www.sf.gov/landlord-petitions-and-passthroughs)).
- **Free Tenant Legal Counsel (`Tenant Right to Counsel` — Proposition F, 2018):** Administered by the SF Mayor's Office of Housing and Community Development (MOHCD) with lead partner Eviction Defense Collaborative and 8+ legal aid organizations, alongside free SF Rent Board counseling/arbitration ([MOHCD Housing Services](https://www.sf.gov/sites/default/files/2024-09/MOHCD%20Pre-Submission%20Webinar%20Slides%20-%209-30-2024%20-%20Housing%20Services.pdf), [SF BOS Res. 001-24](https://sfbos.archive.sf.gov/sites/default/files/r0001-24.pdf)).
