Correct Way to Inform Tenants

Written Notice: Must be in writing, delivered via approved methods [Source: SF.gov]

Notice Period:

  • 30 days for increases under 10% [Source: SF.gov]
  • 90 days for increases of 10% or more [Source: SF.gov]

SF Rent Board Forms: Use official forms (e.g., Form 571) [Source: SF.gov]

Content Requirements: State new rent amount, effective date, and basis for increase [Source: SF.gov]

Reasonable Reasons for Rent Increase

Inflation-Based (CPI): 60% of Bay Area CPI change (1.4% for 2025-26, 1.6% for 2026-27) [Source: SF.gov]

Capital Improvements: Requires Rent Board petition and approval [Source: SF.gov]

Operating Cost Increases: May be petitioned for additional increase [Source: SF.gov]

Property Tax Changes: May be considered in petitions [Source: SF.gov]

Banked Increases: Accumulation from prior years (max 3 years total) [Source: SF.gov]
