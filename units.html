Important notes

  • Unit 1: The April 1, 2026 date is 14 months after the February 1, 2025 lease start and is not an anniversary date, so an increase there resets the anniversary to April 1 and forfeits the 1.4%; the amount available is the 1.6% current annual allowance. sf.gov — Banked increases
  • Unit 1 alternative: Waiting until February 1, 2027 would allow 1.4% + 1.6% = 3.0%, or $119.85, new rent $4,114.85. sf.gov — Irregular periods
  • Unit 2: The 11.7% and 13.1% totals are arithmetic, not a verified current rent, and they depend on which banking interval applies. They also assume no prior increases and the same tenants; any prior increase changes the balance. sf.gov — Banked increases
  • Unit 2 notice: Because both candidate totals exceed 10%, the notice must be at least 90 days, plus 5 days if mailed. Civ. Code §827
  • Additional occupant: A landlord cannot charge additional rent simply because a new roommate moves in with an existing tenant. sf.gov — Rent increases
  • New tenant / banked increases: Banked increases do not carry over to a new tenant. Confirm whether Unit 1's tenancy is continuing or whether a new tenancy began. sf.gov — Banked increases
